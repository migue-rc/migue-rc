## A little about me

I'm a software engineer and data science master's student who enjoys building the whole thing, from a noisy GPS point or messy dataset to a model, API, map, and product that someone can actually use.

Before working in tech, I served in the army as infantry and commando. That experience shaped how I think about training, effort, and resilience, and eventually led me to build **Rucker**.

These days I'm especially curious about movement data, geospatial systems, useful machine learning, and finding good ways to explain what a model is doing.

## What I'm building

### 🥾 [Rucker](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/rucker-the-rucking-app/id6742251218)

I built Rucker because generic fitness apps don't really understand what carrying weight feels like. It smooths noisy GPS data, accounts for terrain, elevation, load, speed, distance, and duration, and uses H3 to turn repeated routes into geospatial heatmaps.

Behind the app: Expo/React Native, Rust, PostgreSQL, Firebase, H3, and Mapbox.

### 🫁 [Lung Cancer Risk Predictor](https://migue-rc.github.io/lung-cancer-risk/)

An experiment in taking a model all the way out of the notebook. I compared logistic and L1-regularized models, explored predictions with SHAP, and moved the final inference into a TypeScript/React app that runs entirely in the browser. [Source code](https://github.com/migue-rc/lung-cancer-risk)

### 🚗 [Auto Insurance & Telematics Analytics](https://github.com/migue-rc/car-insurance)

Two related problems: predicting rare insurance claims and discovering driver behavior from sensor data without using the labels. The project combines imbalanced classification, PCA, K-Means, and interactive visualizations. [Explore the workflow](https://migue-rc.github.io/car-insurance/charts/interactive-flow-chart.html)

### 🎓 [Student Performance Modeling](https://migue-rc.github.io/student-performance/)

A comparison of five regression models, plus PCA and K-Means for exploring student behavior. The selected model reached a test R² of about 0.825 with an MAE of 0.42. [Source code](https://github.com/migue-rc/student-performance)
